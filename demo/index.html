Pricing Glossary (Technical Terms Explained)
Recommended Rate (Final Quoted Rate)
The total hourly rate in KES/hour you quote to the client. It combines your peer-matched base rate with the M-Pesa transfer surcharge so payout fees do not reduce your net earnings.
Base Predicted Rate (Your Take-Home)
Your net hourly earnings before mobile-money transfer fees, calculated from verified peer listings or raised to your Base-Rate Floor if your floor is higher.
M-Pesa Tariff Surcharge
The official M-Pesa transfer fee for your payout band, passed through on top of your base rate.
Base-Rate Floor
Your optional walk-away minimum hourly rate (KES/hour). If the peer estimate or corridor floor falls below this level, the quote is clamped upward to protect your minimum.
Negotiation Corridor (Min – Max Range)
Your recommended bargaining window (excluding the M-Pesa fee). Anchor negotiations near the ceiling for international clients and use the floor as your lowest acceptable retainer rate.
Bilateral Arbitrage Factor
A cross-border purchasing-power index between your country and the client's country. Higher-income client markets widen your upper negotiation ceiling.
Competitiveness Score (0.00 – 1.00)
Measures market crowding in your niche. Lower scores represent rare specializations (wider premium ceiling); higher scores represent crowded markets (tighter corridor around the peer median).
IDW Quote Weight (Inverse Distance Weighting)
The percentage share each comparable peer listing contributed to your base rate. Closer matches in feature space receive exponentially greater weight.
Similarity Score
A 0%–100% profile match score derived from Euclidean distance in the spatial KD-Tree index (1 / (1 + distance)).
Matched Terms (TF-IDF Keywords)
The most distinctive technical terms extracted from your service description that guided peer selection.
Industry Partition & Fallback Routing
Peer listings are grouped by industry domain (e.g., Mobile, Data & AI). If a niche partition has too few verified peers, routing broadens to general_tech so you still receive a reliable quote.
Peer Rate Dispersion (± KES/hour)
The standard deviation across matched peers' hourly rates. Lower dispersion means tighter market consensus.
Confidence (Uncalibrated)
An internal distance diagnostic rather than a calibrated probability. Always rely on peer count and similarity scores to judge evidence strength.
