# M11.3 Kenyan M-Pesa quote correction (#73)

The [canonical blueprint](architecture_blueprint%20%281%29.md#34-safaricom-m-pesa-12-tier-tariff-lookup-table-fr-07) proposes 12 consumer-to-consumer M-Pesa transfer tiers (10–250,000 KES). `tests/fixtures/raw/Mpesa_Tarrifs/tarrifs_full_schedule.csv` models **that proposal for automated tests only**. The proposed table has **not** been independently verified against a dated Safaricom source; tests passing must not be represented as confirmation of current official charges. The older untracked `data/raw/Mpesa_Tarrifs/tarrifs.csv` is also not an approved release input.

## Quote and audit policy

- The protected gateway checks the mentor's Firebase ownership and requires `mentorCountry` to match the authorized mentor document's country. Only verified **KE** mentors get a surcharge; a non-KE mentor's surcharge is zero, irrespective of the client's country.
- All returned rates are in **KES/hour**. `basePredictedRate` is the rounded peer-weighted base. Exactly one configured transfer fee is added: `finalQuotedRate = basePredictedRate + mpesaTariffSurcharge`. These hourly recommendations assume a one-hour transfer for fee illustration, **not** a guarantee about a later payment's actual transaction amount or fee.
- `baseRateFloor` is a reservation floor on the **fee-free** negotiation corridor, not a clamp on the point estimate. Neither `minQuotedRate` nor `maxQuotedRate` includes M-Pesa; therefore a high requested floor may exceed the point quote, or the final quote may exceed the corridor ceiling. An impossible floor returns 422 and schedules no success audit. Comparable peer rates remain fee-free evidence. The response reason states whether a Kenyan fee was added.
- A successful runtime audit records base, surcharge, final rate, verified mentor identity and the configured tariff file SHA-256; no audit is written for rejected quotes. No client-supplied country overrides the verified mentor's country for protected pricing.
- The evaluator preserves the 12 exact, ordered tier ranges and configured fees: no average/blend, overlap repair or last-tier clamping. For cent-valued rates the first cent above a whole-KES upper bound moves to the next tier. A KE rate below 10 KES/hour or above 250,000 KES/hour is unavailable (503, no success audit); non-KE rates bypass tariff lookup. Invalid/missing files or a mismatched pin keep the runtime unready (503).

## Production tariff release gate

Before deploying #73 for real quotes, obtain and **record** an independently reviewable dated Safaricom schedule (source URL/document, retrieval and effective dates, transfer type and channel), compare **all 12 fees and boundaries** with the blueprint, and resolve any discrepancies in #73 rather than silently treating the test fixture as official. If official ranges change, revise and review the expected schema and tests in an issue/PR. Record the approved CSV's SHA-256 in separate release configuration as `PRICING_TARIFF_SHA256` and mount the approved CSV read-only via `PRICING_TARIFF_CSV`; do not calculate the trusted digest from an unreviewed mounted/downloaded file at startup. The protected entrypoint requires the pin; local `src.serve:app` may load a fixture explicitly for development only. See [container delivery](M13.2_Container_and_CI.md).
