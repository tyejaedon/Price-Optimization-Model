## Quote and audit policy

- The protected gateway checks the mentor's Firebase ownership and requires `mentorCountry` to match the authorized mentor document's country. Only verified **KE** mentors get a surcharge; a non-KE mentor's surcharge is zero, irrespective of the client's country.
- All returned rates are in **KES/hour**. `basePredictedRate` is the rounded peer-weighted base. Exactly one configured transfer fee is added: `finalQuotedRate = basePredictedRate + mpesaTariffSurcharge`. These hourly recommendations assume a one-hour transfer for fee illustration, **not** a guarantee about a later payment's actual transaction amount or fee.
- `baseRateFloor` is a reservation floor on the **fee-free** negotiation corridor, not a clamp on the point estimate. Neither `minQuotedRate` nor `maxQuotedRate` includes M-Pesa; therefore a high requested floor may exceed the point quote, or the final quote may exceed the corridor ceiling. An impossible floor returns 422 and schedules no success audit. Comparable peer rates remain fee-free evidence. The response reason states whether a Kenyan fee was added.
- A successful runtime audit records base, surcharge, final rate, verified mentor identity and the configured tariff file SHA-256; no audit is written for rejected quotes. No client-supplied country overrides the verified mentor's country for protected pricing.
- The evaluator preserves the 15 exact, ordered transfer-to-M-PESA-user tier ranges and configured fees: no average/blend, overlap repair or last-tier clamping. For cent-valued rates the first cent above a whole-KES upper bound moves to the next tier. A KE rate below 1 KES/hour or above 250,000 KES/hour is unavailable (503, no success audit); non-KE rates bypass tariff lookup. Invalid/missing files or a mismatched pin keep the runtime unready (503).

## Other M-PESA services: versioned reference only

`config/tariffs/mpesa_other_services_2026-08-04.csv` separately records the supplied **other-network transfers, agent withdrawals, ATM withdrawals, Pochi la Biashara, free services, receiving Buy Goods, and Business Till pay-to-mobile/pay-to-Paybill** tables. Columns identify `service`, `payer`, KES amount bounds, a flat KES fee *or* a percentage (a value of `0.55` means **0.55%**, not KES 0.55), `status` and `validity`. Blank fees mean **not available/unspecified**, not zero. No other-service row can be read by the current P2P evaluator or the pricing API. Buy Goods customers generally pay zero while merchants pay the listed fee; authorised fuel-station charges may instead be borne by customers and require separate rules. The Pochi table says **for three months from August 1, 2026**: do not assume these fees remain in force afterward. Some published Pochi/Till intervals overlap at boundaries; the catalog preserves the page verbatim and deliberately provides **no automatic lookup** for those services. No Visa/bank commissions are recorded because no source, rail, payer, effective date or actual rates were supplied.

Adding a payment rail later is tracked in [#116](https://github.com/tyejaedon/Price-Optimization-Model/issues/116). It requires dated source data, explicit applicability and payer, non-overlapping lookup boundaries, fee unit/percentage and rounding, expiration/version pinning, a new request/response contract and tests. No fee type may be inferred from `mentorCountry` alone or silently charged on a P2P quote.

Before deploying #73 for real quotes, check the live official page's **effective revision, transfer type and all 15 band/fee values** against this transcription and record a reviewer, date and approved file digest in independent release configuration. The page's "last updated" date is not a guarantee against future changes. If official ranges or fees change, revise the versioned CSV, blueprint and tests in a new reviewable issue/PR rather than silently accepting stale data. Record the approved CSV's SHA-256 separately as `PRICING_TARIFF_SHA256` and mount the reviewed P2P CSV read-only via `PRICING_TARIFF_CSV`; do not calculate the trusted digest from an unreviewed mounted/downloaded file at startup.
