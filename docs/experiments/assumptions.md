# Assumptions and parameter provenance

**State as of 2026-10-06.** A configured number is not automatically estimated, optimal or evidence of real mentor-price accuracy. Categories here are **design default**, **limited proxy evidence**, **sourced external schedule**, and **business/contract rule**. Explaining what a parameter *does* does not establish why its **exact number** was originally chosen: unless a linked source records that rationale, treat it as **not established**. The [experiment index](README.md) and original linked reports contain the provenance and split for each measurement. Revisit this table in the relevant issue PR after a completed, independently reviewable experiment; do not rewrite an old experiment's conclusion.

## How to justify a future change

For every variable, distinguish **why the current value was chosen** (constraint, mathematical rule, source or historical convention) from **what was actually measured** (population, snapshot SHA-256, split, comparator, raw-unit metrics, uncertainty and latency). Where the original rationale is not independently documented, record it as **not established**, not as a proven optimum. A research-only proxy winner is a proposal, not a serving decision: open a separate migration issue for altered feature/contract/artifact shapes, train/serve parity, security and protected latency. Preserve rejected candidates and any failure to identify a winner in the linked [experiment record](record_template.md).
